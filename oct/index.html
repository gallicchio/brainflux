More: glass in one arm only (dispersion)
A block of BK7 glass in the sample arm with no twin in the reference arm. Glass delays each colour differently (dispersion), so the colours' fringes no longer line up: the burst spreads and its fringes are chirped. (The mirror is put back by the glass's group delay, so the burst stays where it was.)
